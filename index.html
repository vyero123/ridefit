Learning when to shift

Six drills, each one isolating a single thing. No lap times, no score for going fast — the only thing measured here is whether you changed gear at the right moment and what you did with the clutch.

If you ride, you already have the instincts for revs and for a clutch. The part that does not transfer is that a car's clutch is dry: no oil, no cooling, and much less tolerance for being slipped. Drill 5 and the reference section are about that.

One invented, clearly stated car. Real shift points vary by engine, gearbox, load and gradient, so everything here is a range and a principle, not your car's numbers. For use at home — never while driving.

Clutch heat

The clutch and throttle follow your finger: where you hold it is where the pedal is, and lifting off is taking your foot off. On a keyboard, space clutch, X throttle and Z brake go all the way down; the lever takes the arrow keys once you tap it.
