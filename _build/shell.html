One seat per rider — and per dog: a dog takes a seat like a passenger. A toddler in a car seat takes a rear seat (never the front); a stroller rides folded in the trunk, cargo bay or bed and comes out of the space dogs could use. When the seats run out it rides in the cargo bay (if the published space is big enough) or the bed of a pickup; a sedan's trunk doesn't count. Seating order: you drive, the second adult rides up front, everyone else fills the rear rows, and a front bench middle seat is used last (by a dog before a person). Cats, as everyone knows, take nothing and go wherever they please. Seat counts are the standard seating for the trim shown.
